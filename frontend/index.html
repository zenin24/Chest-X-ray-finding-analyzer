Clinical Guidance & Safe AI Practice

1. Multimodal Similarity vs. Diagnostic Probability

PubMedCLIP outputs are normalized cosine similarities between the image visual embedding and medical prompt text embeddings. A score of 0.78 denotes high embedding proximity in biomedical feature space — not an empirical 78% prevalence probability.

2. Zero-Shot Generalization Boundaries

The zero-shot paradigm avoids fine-tuning overfitting but may exhibit sensitivity to projection view (PA vs. AP), patient rotation, foreign bodies (pacemakers, endotracheal tubes), or severe scoliosis.

3. Spatial Patch Attribution Interpretation

The 7×7 spatial attribution matrix highlights the ViT-B/32 vision encoder tokens with the greatest cosine alignment to the finding prompt. It serves as visual attribution and should not be used as an exact anatomical segmentation boundary.

4. Human-in-the-Loop Requirement

This tool is classified as a Clinical Decision Support (CDS) Research Prototype. All candidate findings must be reviewed, confirmed, or dismissed by a board-certified radiologist or qualified physician.
